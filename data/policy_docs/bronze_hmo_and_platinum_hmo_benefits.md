# Meridian Health Plan — Bronze HMO & Platinum HMO Benefit Summary

*Fictional plan document generated for a portfolio project. Not a real insurance product.*

## Bronze HMO
Lowest monthly premium, highest cost-sharing. Requires a designated primary care provider (PCP) and referrals for all specialist visits. No out-of-network coverage except true emergencies.

- Annual deductible: $6,000 individual / $12,000 family
- Out-of-pocket maximum: $8,700 individual / $17,400 family
- Primary care visit: $40 copay
- Specialist visit (with referral): subject to deductible, then $80 copay
- Behavioral health: subject to deductible, then $50 copay; prior authorization required after 8 visits per plan year
- Imaging (MRI, CT): subject to deductible, 40% coinsurance after; **prior authorization required**

## Platinum HMO
Highest monthly premium, lowest cost-sharing. Requires a designated PCP and referrals for specialist visits, but most services have minimal copays and no deductible.

- Annual deductible: $0
- Out-of-pocket maximum: $2,000 individual / $4,000 family
- Primary care visit: $10 copay
- Specialist visit (with referral): $20 copay
- Behavioral health: $10 copay per visit, no prior authorization required for outpatient therapy
- Imaging (MRI, CT): $50 copay; **prior authorization still required** for scheduling, though no coinsurance applies
- Specialty pharmacy (Tier 4 drugs): $75 copay, prior authorization required

## HMO Referral Rule
Both Bronze HMO and Platinum HMO require an active PCP referral on file for any specialist visit to be paid. Claims without a referral on file are denied under reason code **CO-197** (treated the same as a missing prior authorization).
