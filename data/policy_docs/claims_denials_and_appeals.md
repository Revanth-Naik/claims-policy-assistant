# Meridian Health Plan — Claim Denials & Appeals Policy

*Fictional plan document generated for a portfolio project. Not a real insurance product.*

## Denial Reason Codes
| Code | Meaning |
|------|---------|
| CO-50 | Service not deemed a medical necessity based on submitted diagnosis |
| CO-97 | Charge is bundled into the reimbursement of another already-paid service |
| PR-1 | Member's annual deductible has not yet been met; amount is member responsibility |
| CO-197 | Required precertification or prior authorization was not on file at time of service |
| CO-29 | Claim was submitted after the timely filing limit (180 days from service date) |

## Appeals Process
A member or provider may appeal a denied claim within 180 days of the denial notice.

1. **Level 1 (Internal Review):** Submit a written appeal with supporting clinical documentation. Meridian issues a decision within 30 calendar days for standard appeals, or 72 hours for urgent/expedited appeals involving an active course of treatment.
2. **Level 2 (Peer Review):** If the Level 1 appeal is upheld, the member may request a second review by a physician in the same or similar specialty who was not involved in the original decision. Decision issued within 30 calendar days.
3. **External Review:** If both internal levels are exhausted, the member may request an independent external review through the state's designated external review organization. This decision is binding.

## Notes on CO-197 Denials Specifically
The majority of CO-197 denials are resolved at Level 1 by retroactively submitting the missing prior authorization or referral documentation, provided the service itself met medical necessity criteria. Providers are encouraged to verify authorization status *before* rendering non-emergency services covered under the Prior Authorization list in each plan's benefit summary.
