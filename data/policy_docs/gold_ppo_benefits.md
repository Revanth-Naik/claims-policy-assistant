# Meridian Health Plan — Gold PPO Benefit Summary

*Fictional plan document generated for a portfolio project. Not a real insurance product.*

## Overview
The Meridian Gold PPO plan offers broad access to in-network and out-of-network providers with moderate cost-sharing. Members may see any licensed specialist without a referral.

## Cost Sharing
- Annual deductible: $750 individual / $1,500 family (in-network)
- Out-of-pocket maximum: $4,500 individual / $9,000 family
- Primary care visit: $25 copay
- Specialist visit: $50 copay
- Emergency room: $250 copay (waived if admitted)
- Out-of-network care: covered at 60% after deductible is met

## Covered Services
- Preventive care (annual physicals, screenings, immunizations): covered at 100%, no deductible
- Behavioral health (outpatient therapy, psychiatry): $30 copay per visit, no prior authorization required for the first 12 visits per plan year
- Physical therapy: $30 copay, up to 30 visits per plan year
- Imaging (MRI, CT): subject to deductible + 20% coinsurance; **prior authorization required** for all non-emergency MRI and CT scans
- Durable medical equipment: 20% coinsurance after deductible
- Specialty pharmacy (Tier 4 drugs): 25% coinsurance, prior authorization required

## Prior Authorization
The following services require prior authorization before the service date. Claims for these services submitted without an approved authorization on file will be denied with reason code **CO-197**.
- Inpatient hospital admissions (non-emergency)
- MRI and CT imaging
- Specialty pharmacy (Tier 3 and Tier 4 drugs)
- Durable medical equipment over $1,000
- Out-of-network referrals for non-emergency specialist care

## Network
Gold PPO members receive the highest level of benefit when using Meridian's in-network provider directory. Out-of-network services are covered at a reduced coinsurance rate and are subject to balance billing.
