# Meridian Health Plan — Silver PPO Benefit Summary

*Fictional plan document generated for a portfolio project. Not a real insurance product.*

## Overview
The Silver PPO plan balances lower monthly premiums with moderate cost-sharing at the point of care. Like Gold PPO, no referral is required to see specialists.

## Cost Sharing
- Annual deductible: $2,000 individual / $4,000 family (in-network)
- Out-of-pocket maximum: $7,000 individual / $14,000 family
- Primary care visit: $35 copay
- Specialist visit: $70 copay
- Emergency room: $350 copay (waived if admitted)
- Out-of-network care: covered at 50% after deductible is met

## Covered Services
- Preventive care: covered at 100%, no deductible
- Behavioral health: subject to deductible, then $40 copay per visit; prior authorization required after 12 visits per plan year
- Physical therapy: subject to deductible, then $40 copay, up to 20 visits per plan year
- Imaging (MRI, CT): subject to deductible + 30% coinsurance; **prior authorization required** for all non-emergency MRI and CT scans
- Specialty pharmacy (Tier 4 drugs): 30% coinsurance, prior authorization required

## Prior Authorization
Same prior-authorization list as Gold PPO applies (inpatient admissions, MRI/CT, Tier 3-4 specialty pharmacy, DME over $1,000, out-of-network specialist referrals). Claims for these services without an approved authorization are denied under reason code **CO-197**.
